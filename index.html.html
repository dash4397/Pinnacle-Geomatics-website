About the Firm

Pinnacle Geomatics and Engineering Services Limited is a full-service geomatics and engineering consultancy based in Ibadan, Oyo State, providing accurate, standards-compliant survey and spatial data services across South-West Nigeria.

We work with individual landowners, developers, estate agencies and institutions who need to know — with certainty — where a boundary sits, what a title actually covers, and how a project should be sequenced from ground-breaking to close-out. Our approach ties every service back to verified field data, so a survey plan, a title opinion and a project schedule are never working from different numbers.
